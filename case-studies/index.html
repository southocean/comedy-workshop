Other people's sets, same instrument

Take a bit you love, mark up its setups, punchlines and tags, and write down the expectation it was steering the room into. If you cannot name the axis, you have not understood the joke yet - you have only enjoyed it.

On material that is not yours. These are other comedians' copyrighted acts. Store only the short beats you are actually annotating, always credited, and keep the file in this private repo. The value here is the mark-up, not the transcript.
