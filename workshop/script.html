The orange S marker is a point of subversion - hover or click the line to see what the room expected, what they got, and the axis between the two. Setups show the full distribution of expectations with a concentration grade. The gutter time is what that single line costs, including its pause and its budgeted laugh.

Cuttable lines toggles the marked-optional beats and recalculates the runtime, so you can see what the set weighs after trimming. Plain text strips every annotation down to a clean rehearsal script. Pace and room re-run the estimate at a different speaking speed and a different assumption about how much the audience actually laughs - drop the room to 60% to see the set as it will run on a quiet Tuesday.
